06 — Usage

Usage

Guardrails that keep the mark sharp and consistent everywhere it appears.

Clear space

Keep the dashed zone free of type, graphics, and other elements — on every side of the mark.

Clear space zone

Minimum size

Never render the mark smaller than the sizes below — legibility collapses before then.

Digital — emblem at 24 px height Screens, UI, signatures
Print — lockup at 10 mm width Business cards, letterheads

Do's & Don'ts

Do

  • Use the approved palette only.
  • Keep the clear space around every version.
  • Pair Doran & Estedad for Farsi, Roboto & Inter Tight for English.
  • Recolor patterns only within the corporate colors.

Don't

  • Stretch, distort, or rotate the mark.
  • Recolor the mark outside the palette.
  • Add shadows, outlines, or effects.
  • Place the mark on busy backgrounds.
  • Redraw or reconstruct the mark.

Patterns may be recolored only with the brand's corporate colors — any other modification is not permitted.
